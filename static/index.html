Google Sheets & Drive 1-Click Sync Setup

Automate live queue tracking, payment status & Google Drive photo uploads
Why is this needed? Google blocks external apps from sending direct POST data to spreadsheet links. Google Apps Script takes 60 seconds to set up and automatically:
  • Appends rows with Timestamp, Queue #, Guest Name, Payment Status, and Print Handed Status.
  • Creates a dedicated session folder in your Google Drive Folder (e.g. Queue #16 - Guest - 2026-10-04 23-19-03) for every photobooth session.
  • Uploads the Final Ticket, all RAW Camera Photos, and the RAW Video Recording directly into the session folder!
  • Adds direct Google Drive session folder links right in your Google Sheet!
